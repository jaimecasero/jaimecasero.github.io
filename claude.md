Set of simple web applications related to music theory and practice.


All based on basic html/css/javascript. Designed for mobile phones as simgle page apps streching to full vertical screen. to be used with thumb finger mainly(most input ar bottom of screen)

./berimbot is a capoeira beat generator for practicing
./drumtice midi to drum sheet conversion with play along features
./earforge develops your music ear by playing different games with intervals/chords/melodies
./fifths cicle of fifths app allowing to select keys modes, and showing diatonic chords
./staff midi to staff converter with on screen/midi/voice input to play the loaded song
